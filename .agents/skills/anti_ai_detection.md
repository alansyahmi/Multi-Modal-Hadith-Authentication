# Skill: Anti-AI Detection (Perplexity & Burstiness Optimization)

This skill provides instructions for generating or rewriting text to minimize the probability of detection by AI classifiers like Turnitin. It focuses on maximizing two key metrics: **Perplexity** and **Burstiness**.

## Core Principles

### 1. Maximize Perplexity (Predictability Reduction)
Perplexity measures how "surprising" a word choice is to a statistical model. AI tends to choose the most likely next word, resulting in low perplexity.

*   **Diverse Vocabulary**: Avoid overused AI-favored words (e.g., "delve," "tapestry," "comprehensive," "essential"). Use specific, context-heavy synonyms.
*   **Atypical Transitions**: Replace standard AI connectors ("Furthermore," "In conclusion," "Additionally") with more organic, narrative-driven transitions or direct thematic links.
*   **Idiomatic & Nuanced Phrasing**: Use natural-sounding idioms, metaphors, and phrasing that reflects personal or specialized expertise rather than generic summaries.
*   **Intentional "Human" Flaws**: Incorporate subtle stylistic choices like parenthetical asides `(like this one)`, rhetorical questions, or slightly non-linear reasoning that follows a train of thought rather than a rigid template.

### 2. Maximize Burstiness (Structural Variation)
Burstiness measures the variation in sentence length and complexity. AI often produces a "steady" rhythm of medium-length sentences.

*   **Rhythmic Contrast**: Intentionally place a very short, punchy sentence immediately after or before a long, complex, multi-clause sentence.
*   **Variable Openers**: Never start three consecutive sentences with the same type of word (e.g., Subject-Verb). Start with prepositional phrases, dependent clauses, or adverbs.
*   **Complex Punctuation**: Use em-dashes (—) for emphasis, semicolons (;) to link related thoughts, and parentheses for additional context. This creates a more "jagged" and human-like structural profile.
*   **Paragraph Dynamics**: Vary paragraph length. Use a single-sentence paragraph for impact, followed by a more descriptive one.

---

## Instructions for Execution

When asked to apply this skill to a piece of writing:

1.  **Analyze the Tone**: Maintain the user's intended tone (academic, professional, casual) while increasing the variance.
2.  **The "Burst" Test**: Check that sentence lengths vary by at least 15-20 words within the same paragraph.
3.  **The "Predictability" Test**: Scan for "AI-isms" (perfectly balanced lists of three, generic introductions, repetitive "Firstly/Secondly") and replace them with unique structural approaches.
4.  **Refine**: Ensure the text remains coherent and high-quality. The goal is to sound like a highly skilled human writer, not an incoherent one.

## Example Transformation

**Original (AI-style):**
The implementation of renewable energy sources is essential for the environment. Furthermore, solar panels provide a sustainable way to generate electricity. This technology helps reduce carbon emissions and promotes a cleaner future for everyone.

**Optimized (Humanized):**
Transitioning from fossil fuels to renewables is not just a policy choice, rather, it is a vital initiation for the environment. Solar panels for instance represent a fundamental shift in how we harvest power from the sun itself. By reducing carbon footprints, this transition effectively helps to promote a cleaner future for everyone.
