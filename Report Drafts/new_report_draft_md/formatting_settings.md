# Thesis Formatting & Transliteration Settings

This document compiles the formatting rules, paragraph layouts, and Arabic transliteration guidelines for Chapter 3.

---

## 1. Document Layout & Page Settings

* **Margins**: Standard 1.0 inch (2.54 cm) on all sides (Top, Bottom, Left, Right).
* **Page Size**: A4 size.
* **Line Spacing**: 1.5 line spacing for chapter body text.
* **Paragraph Alignment**: Justified for body text to maintain clean academic margins.
* **Paragraph Indentation**:
  - Every paragraph after the **second paragraph** in any section must be indented.
  - The indentation size must reach exactly **0.5 inches** (including first line indents, lists, and numbers).

---

## 2. Heading Configurations

* **Font & Style**: All headings must be **BOLD**, size **11pt** (or 11px), **Tahoma**, color **Black**.
* **Heading Levels**:
  - **Heading 1**: 3.x sections (e.g. `3.1 Research Design`). Centered, Bold, Tahoma Black, 11pt.
  - **Heading 2**: 3.x.x sections (e.g. `3.2.1 Data Sources`). Left-aligned, Bold, Tahoma Black, 11pt.
  - **Heading 3**: 3.x.x.x sections (e.g. `3.2.3.1 Tier 1: Heuristic Search Space Reduction`). Left-aligned, Bold, Tahoma Black, 11pt.
  - **Chapter Title**: Centered, Bold, Tahoma Black, 11pt (e.g. `CHAPTER 3: PROJECT METHODOLOGY`).

---

## 3. Font Selection & Encoding

* **Body Font Family**: **Tahoma**, Size: 11pt, Color: Black.
* **Code Font Family**: **Lucida Console** (or Lucadia Consolas), Size: 11px (8.25pt), Color: Black. Used for all coding parts, script filenames, flowcharts, and configuration blocks.

---

## 4. Equations & Tables

* **Equations**: Centered and auto-converted using built-in Word equation functions.
* **Tables**:
  - Style: Super basic default table theme using thin black lines.
  - Content Font: Normal text (Tahoma, 11pt), with **Bold** formatting applied strictly to the **first row** (headers).
  - Title and Captions/Comments: Placed strictly on **top of the table** and **centered** (instead of justified).

---

## 5. Foreign Terms & Arabic Transliteration (Wehr Transcription Style)

Assimilated English terms (e.g., Hadith, Quran) follow standard English conventions without special diacritics. Non-assimilated or highly technical Arabic terms must strictly follow the **Hans Wehr Transliteration System** and be **Italicized**:

### A. Consonants and Vowels
* **Ayn (`ع`)**: Represented by the left half-ring character `ʿ` (U+02BF). Do not use simple apostrophes (`'`).
* **Hamza (`ء`)**: Represented by the right half-ring character `ʾ` (U+02BE) when occurring mid-word.
* **Long Vowels**:
  - Ā/ā (Alif): `ā` (e.g., *isnād*, *Ṣaḥāba*, *Tābiʿūn*)
  - Ī/ī (Ya): `ī` (e.g., *Ṣaḥīḥ*, *Ḍaʿīf*, *taʿdīl*)
  - Ū/ū (Waw): `ū` (e.g., *Maqbul*, *Tābiʿūn*)
* **Special Consonants**:
  - `ḥ` (Hā `ح` with dot below): *Jarḥ*, *Ṣaḥīḥ*, *Ḥasan*
  - `ṣ` / `Ṣ` (Sād `ص` with dot below): *Ṣaḥīḥ*, *Ṣaḥāba*
  - `ḍ` / `Ḍ` (Dād `ض` with dot below): *Ḍaʿīf*, *Mawḍūʿ*
  - `š` (Shīn `ش` with caron/haček): *šāḏ*
  - `ḏ` (Dhāl `ذ` with line under): *šāḏ*
  - `ṯ` (Thā `ث` with line under)
  - `ʿ` (Ayn): *ʿIlm ar-Rijal*, *Jarḥ wa Taʿdīl*, *Tābiʿūn*, *Ḍaʿīf*, *Mawḍūʿ*
